📖 Reading — A new weekend plan

A Weekend Without a Car

Last summer, Daniel moved to a small town near Bristol because he got a new job. At first, he was worried. He didn't have a car, and the nearest supermarket was three kilometres away. However, he soon discovered that life without a car could be easier than he expected.

On weekdays, Daniel usually cycles to work. It takes twenty minutes, which is faster than the bus during rush hour. On Fridays, he works from home, so he doesn't need to travel. At weekends, he meets his friend Leo, who lives in the city. They often go hiking or visit local markets.

Last Saturday, they wanted to visit a lake, but the weather changed suddenly. Instead of cancelling their plans, they took a train to a nearby village and explored an old castle. Daniel had never visited it before. The tickets were a little expensive, but the view from the top was amazing.

Now Daniel is thinking about buying an electric bicycle. He hasn't decided yet because he wants to save money for a holiday abroad. Although travelling without a car sometimes takes longer, Daniel says it has helped him become healthier and discover new places.
