2 · Share an invitation

Create a room, then send your partner the Webilang invitation link. You both see the same activity and join the same video room.

Your invitation link will appear here after you create a room.

How it works

  1. Create a room and copy the invitation.
  2. Share the link with one or more partners.
  3. Join your room and allow microphone/camera access.
  4. Talk, solve a problem, or practise English and Russian.
✨ Tip: In a bilingual exchange, agree to speak English first and Russian second. Try 10 minutes per language.
